Paroxetine DGI profiles assess CYP2D6 activity-score- and phenotype-dependent paroxetine exposure. Studies are interpreted separately because their dose, regimen, and sampling schedules differ.

| **Study** | **Route and dose** | **CYP2D6 groups** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Chen 2015](#references) | Oral paroxetine hydrochloride, 25 mg single dose | AS 0.5, 1, 1.5, and 2 |
| [Mürdter 2016](#references) | Oral paroxetine hydrochloride, 40 mg multiple dose | AS 0, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2, and 3 |
| [Sindrup 1992](#references) | Oral paroxetine hydrochloride, 30 mg multiple dose | PM and NM |
| [Yoon 2000](#references) | Oral paroxetine hydrochloride, 40 mg single dose | AS 0, 0.5, 1.25, and 2 |

AS: CYP2D6 activity score. NM: normal metabolizer. PM: poor metabolizer.
