The desipramine DGI evaluation uses phenotype-stratified parent-model studies to assess CYP2D6-dependent desipramine exposure. Comparisons are made within each study because dose, route, and sampling duration differ.

| **Study** | **Route and dose** | **CYP2D6 groups** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Brøsen 1986](#references) | Oral desipramine hydrochloride, 100 mg single dose | PM, NM, and fast NM |
| [Brøsen 1988](#references) | Intravenous desipramine hydrochloride, 50 mg single dose | PM, NM, and fast NM |
| [Spina 1987](#references) | Oral desipramine hydrochloride, 25 mg single dose | PM and NM |

PM: poor metabolizer. NM: normal metabolizer.
