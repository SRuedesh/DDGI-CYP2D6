Atomoxetine DGI profiles assess CYP2D6 activity-score-dependent atomoxetine exposure. Studies are interpreted separately because their doses and activity-score groups differ.

| **Study** | **Route and dose** | **CYP2D6 groups** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Byeon 2015](#references) | Oral atomoxetine, 40 mg dose-normalized | AS 0.5, 1.25, and 2 |
| [Kim 2018](#references) | Oral atomoxetine, 20 mg single dose | AS 0.5 and 2 |

AS: CYP2D6 activity score.
