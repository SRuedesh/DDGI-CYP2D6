Dextromethorphan DGI profiles assess CYP2D6-dependent parent-drug exposure and dextrorphan formation. Studies are interpreted separately because dose, formulation, and phenotype grouping differ.

| **Study** | **Route and dose** | **CYP2D6 groups** | **Analyte(s)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Capon 1996](#references) | Oral dextromethorphan hydrobromide, 30 mg single dose | PM and NM | Dextromethorphan |
| [Gorski 2004](#references) | Oral dextromethorphan hydrobromide, 30 mg single dose | PM and NM | Dextromethorphan |
| [Qiu 2016](#references) | Oral dextromethorphan hydrobromide, 15 mg single dose | AS 0.5, 1.25, and 2 | Dextromethorphan |
| [Storelli 2018](#references) | Oral dextromethorphan base, 5 mg single dose | IM and NM | Dextromethorphan and dextrorphan |

IM: intermediate metabolizer. NM: normal metabolizer. PM: poor metabolizer.
